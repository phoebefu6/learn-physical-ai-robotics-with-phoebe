# Learn Physical AI & Robotics with Phoebe

Sixteen 45-minute sessions on physical AI and robotics as a data problem: what changes when a model's output moves a physical arm.

- **Leader track (a1-a6):** the robot as a data loop, demonstrations as the dataset, what a success rate can and cannot tell you, reading a robot demo claim, why the warehouse playbook breaks here, and a transfer map for data people.
- **Practitioner track (p1-p10):** episodes, coordinate frames and calibration, teleoperation data quality, mixing data across robots, actions as data, domain randomization, evaluating a policy in 20 trials, intervention logs, stop logs, and the sim-to-real bench.

Wrenfield Lab, the running case, is constructed. The bench (`assets/pai-live.js`) trains a tiny grasp policy in the browser on seeded synthetic episodes and checks itself on every load. Research is cited by author and year on each page. Not investment, legal or compliance advice.

Static HTML, no build step: open `index.html`.
